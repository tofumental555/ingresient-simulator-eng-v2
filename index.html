📋 Calculation Assumptions & Notes
[Calculation assumptions]
・ Assumes energy is always kept at 81% or higher
・ Assumes tapping every minute (frequent logins)
・ Assumes ingredient inventory never overflows
・ Calculated for 24 hours a day, fully active for 7 days
[About the Helping Speed bonus]
・ Helping Speed M (+14%) and Helping Speed S (+7%) only apply to the Pokémon that has them
・ Helping Bonus (+5%) raises the speed of every Pokémon on the team while it's on the team
・ The bonus applied to each Pokémon = its own Helping Speed M/S + the team's total Helping Bonus
・ If this total exceeds 35%, it's capped at 35%
・ Example: 3 Helping Bonus Pokémon on the team (+15%) + your own Helping Speed M+S (+21%) = 36% → capped at 35%
・ Since ingredient Pokémon rotate in and out, Helping Bonus is prorated by (slots ÷ registered Pokémon)
[Other notes]
・ Ingredient find rate = base rate × (1 + Ingredient Finder M×36% + S×18%)
・ Using a Good Camp Ticket multiplies Helping Speed by ×1.2
・ Starting-of-week stock (ingredients carried over from last week) can be entered on the dish registration screen
・ These results are estimates and may differ slightly from the actual game
[About inventory overflow]
・ 1 day = 15.5 awake hours (tap every minute, no overflow) + 8.5 sleep hours
・ If "tap every minute during sleep" is OFF, once inventory is full,
　no more ingredients are gathered and the berry rate becomes 100% (ingredients are lost)
・ Supports the Inventory Up S (+6), M (+12), and L (+18) sub-skills
・ Pokémon with no registered max-inventory data skip the overflow calculation
[About Ingredient Select]
・ For Ingredient Select Pokémon, skill level is assumed to be at max level
・ With no tapping during sleep, skill activations are calculated as:
　up to 1/day for Berry/Ingredient types, up to 2/day for Skill types
